## Values without a direct source

Every parameter tagged `nominal` in the app, and the scalar torso transfer factors, are teaching or modelling choices. They are not measurements. This covers the autonomic coefficients, AV nodal recovery amplitude/τ/ERP, bundle and Purkinje CVs, PMJ delay, anisotropy ratio, ERP/APD ratios, smoothing radius, the APD floor and CV restitution.

## Phase 2 additions
- ten Tusscher KHWJ, Panfilov AV. Alternans and spiral breakup in a human ventricular tissue model. Am J Physiol Heart Circ Physiol 2006;291:H1088–H1100. doi:10.1152/ajpheart.00109.2006 — TP06 equations as distributed in cbcbeat (gotran/CellML translation).
- Bartolucci C, Passini E, Hyttinen J, Paci M, Severi S. Simulation of the effects of extracellular calcium changes leads to a novel computational model of human ventricular action potential with a revised calcium handling. Front Physiol 2020;11:314. doi:10.3389/fphys.2020.00314 — most human AP models (including ORd) fail to reproduce the inverse APD–[Ca²⁺]o dependence; the basis for marking calcium disorders unsupported.
- Sun J, Amellal F, Glass L, Billette J. Alternans and period-doubling bifurcations in atrioventricular nodal conduction. J Theor Biol 1995;173:79–91 — AV-nodal recovery term reused for the slow pathway.
- Cable-theory relation CV ∝ √(dV/dt_max) used for the ionic→tissue CV mapping is an approximation (uniform fibre); no numerical calibration to human data is claimed.
- Accessory-pathway, slow-pathway and ectopy parameters are nominal (no data source); they are chosen to demonstrate mechanisms.

## Phase 3 additions (electrolytes, triggered activity, drugs, ventricular arrhythmias)
- Young WJ, Warren HR, Mook-Kanamori DO, et al. Genetically determined serum calcium levels and markers of ventricular repolarization. *Circ Genom Precis Med* 2021;14:e003231. doi:10.1161/CIRCGEN.120.003231 — Mendelian randomisation, UK Biobank, n = 76 226: QT +3.01 ms (95% CI 2.03–3.99) and JT +2.89 ms per 0.1 mmol/L **decrease** in total serum calcium, with no clinically relevant QRS association. This is the slope used by the hyper-/hypocalcaemia scenarios (30.1 ms per mmol/L). The same paper reports the observational meta-analysis estimate of 2.23 ms per 0.1 mmol/L (n = 153 014).
- Kim ED, Watt J, Tereshchenko LG, et al. Associations of serum and dialysate electrolytes with QT interval and prolongation in incident hemodialysis: the PACE study. *BMC Nephrol* 2019;20:133. doi:10.1186/s12882-019-1282-5 — QTc +8.55 ms (95% CI 2.13–14.97) per 0.1 mmol/L decrease in **ionised** calcium; quoted as the upper end of the plausible slope and the reason the scenarios probably understate severe hypocalcaemia.
- Bartolucci C, Passini E, Hyttinen J, Paci M, Severi S. *Front Physiol* 2020;11:314. doi:10.3389/fphys.2020.00314 — why TP06/ORd/Grandi invert the APD–[Ca²⁺]o relationship (L-type calcium-dependent inactivation operating in its saturated range).
- Vandersickel N, Kazbanov IV, Nuitermans A, Weise LD, Pandit R, Panfilov AV. A study of early afterdepolarizations in a model for human ventricular tissue. *PLoS ONE* 2014;9:e84595. doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0084595 — EADs are obtained in TP06 by increasing ICaL and reducing the delayed-rectifier K⁺ currents **after halving the ICaL f-gate time constant τ_f**; the basis of the EAD configuration here.
- Chakraborty S, Pandit R, et al. Multiscale studies of delayed afterdepolarizations I/II. arXiv:2307.10134, arXiv:2307.10113 — DADs in TP06 require an added background RyR SR Ca²⁺ leak (V_RyRL = 1.8 × 10⁻⁴ ms⁻¹) on top of calcium overload; the basis of the DAD configuration here.
- Crumb WJ, Vicente J, Johannesen L, Strauss DG. An evaluation of 30 clinical drugs against the comprehensive in vitro proarrhythmia assay (CiPA) proposed ion channel panel. *J Pharmacol Toxicol Methods* 2016;81:251–262. doi:10.1016/j.vascn.2016.03.009 — manual-patch-clamp IC50 values (hERG, Cav1.2, Nav1.5) and free therapeutic plasma concentrations used for all drug scenarios. Hill coefficients are not reported there; h = 1 is assumed and flagged.
- Nair K, Farid T, Masse S, et al. Studying semblances of a true killer: experimental model of human ventricular fibrillation. *Am J Physiol Heart Circ Physiol* 2012;302:H1533–H1537. doi:10.1152/ajpheart.00471.2011 — human VF mean cycle length 218 ± 24 ms in vivo (closed chest, defibrillation-threshold testing) and 225 ± 13 ms ex vivo; 9.6 ± 5 endocardial phase singularities. VF calibration target.
- Bradley CP, Clayton RH, Nash MP, et al. Human ventricular fibrillation during global ischemia and reperfusion: paradoxical changes in activation rate and wavefront complexity. *Circ Arrhythm Electrophysiol* 2011;4:684–691. doi:10.1161/CIRCEP.110.961284 — in vivo human epicardial VF: dominant frequency 6.4 Hz before ischaemia falling to 4.7 Hz, 7.7–10.3 phase singularities. Second VF calibration target.
- Dessertenne F. La tachycardie ventriculaire à deux foyers opposés variables. *Arch Mal Coeur Vaiss* 1966;59:263–272 — the original description of torsades de pointes as a tachycardia arising from two alternating opposite foci; the mechanism used by the torsades scenario. (Cited from the secondary literature; the 1966 article itself was not read.)
- Kozhevnikov DO, Yamamoto K, Robotis D, Restivo M, El-Sherif N. Electrophysiological mechanism of enhanced susceptibility of hypertrophied heart to acquired torsade de pointes arrhythmias: tridimensional mapping of activation and recovery patterns. *Circulation* 2002;105:1128–1134. doi:10.1161/hc0902.104711 — torsades initiated by subendocardial focal activity meeting an increased transmural dispersion of repolarisation, producing functional block and re-entry; the alternative (re-entrant) account, which this engine could not reproduce.
- Himel HD, Cupelli M, Boutjdir M, El-Sherif N. Voltage/calcium uncoupling underlies sustained torsade de pointes ventricular tachyarrhythmia in an experimental model of long QT syndrome. *Front Physiol* 2021;12:617847. doi:10.3389/fphys.2021.617847 — most torsades episodes are self-terminating, a minority sustained; the basis for modelling the scenario as recurrent self-terminating runs.

## Phase 6 additions (M cells, torsades re-examination, infarct phases)
- Akar FG, Yan GX, Antzelevitch C, Rosenbaum DS.
